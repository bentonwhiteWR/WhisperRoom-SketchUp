Interior Lights: a panel for the rig already in the model

This is a mockup of a new window that opens straight from the WhisperRoom panel. You don't need to select a room first. It is clickable. Drag a master slider and it rescales every light in that room's rig. Open Adjust by type to change one kind of light at a time. Nothing is sent to V-Ray until you let go of the slider. The log on the right shows what that write would do, including the check-and-repair after V-Ray's drift.
