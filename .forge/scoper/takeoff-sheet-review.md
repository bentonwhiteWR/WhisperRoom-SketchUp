## 1. What the sheet is today

Generated by `python scripts/takeoff-check.py <takeoff.json> --html --embed-photos` from
eval case `blind-f-mech` (one room, four walls, one door, two heaters, five flagged values).
`#autotest` in headless Chrome: `AUTOTEST OK (1 viewers)` (observed).

Top to bottom (observed, `current-desktop-1280.png`):

1. Header: "Take-off review", job, title; a `source:` line holding the file path, image
   names and anchor. Unit toggle.
2. The **ASSUMED / DEFAULT inventory** — five bullets of `mech run 0 — ASSUMED 17'-8": <full
   reason string>`, then "Each of these builds with an ASSUMED note placed in the model at
   the feature itself."
3. Per room: title bar with APPROVE / NEEDS CHANGES; the **photo** (left, ~560 px) beside the
   **"Pen callout → take-off value" ledger** (right, 340 px); then the **plan** (left, fixed
   560 px SVG with zoom bar) beside the **"Stated values — click one to change it" table**
   plus three green **OK cards** and three amber **INTERPRETATION — CONFIRM cards** (which
   are the room's pen notes); then a 340-px **3D viewer**.
4. **STEP 3 — copy this and paste it back to Claude**: patch textarea, COPY PATCH, and a
   line telling the reader to run `python scripts/takeoff-check.py ... --apply-patch`.
5. Sticky footer with counts.

Everything is 9.5–11.5 px Consolas on a card grid; the artifact renders dark or light.

## 2. What is wrong with it as a working surface

Ordered by how much it costs Gabe per review. W1–W3 are the three that matter.

**W1 — The photo is not there when the file is not called `IMG_*`, and the generator says it
is.** `load_photos` and `room_images` (`scripts/takeoff-check.py:2155-2246`) only recognise
sources matching `IMG_[A-Za-z0-9]+\.(jpe?g|png|webp)`. For this case (`sources:
["photo.png"]`, `src: "pen photo.png"`) the console printed *"client photos EMBEDDED"* while
the sheet shows *"no photo source recorded for this room"* (observed, both copies kept). A
scan, a PDF export, a renamed phone shot or anything Gabe saves as `plan.png` silently loses
the comparison the sheet exists for. Data-side fix; see D-a below. Related (audit D-10): the
skill's STOP command omits `--embed-photos`, so by the book the panel is empty even for
`IMG_*` files.

**W2 — The same number is on the page four times and none of the four is a checklist.** Every
value appears in the inventory (if flagged), the ledger, the stated-values table and the plan
(plus the 3D overlay). The ledger and the table sit in different rows of the room, 600 px
apart, so "is 8'-10" the east wall?" means reading the ledger, scrolling to the table to
click it, and scrolling back to the photo — the photo is not visible from the table
(observed at 1280×900: the first screen ends at the ledger). Nothing lets Gabe mark a value
as checked, so there is no sense of progress and no way to know he is done.

**W3 — The five things that actually need his attention are prose at the top, far from where
they are answered.** The inventory is a bulleted paragraph per flag with the full reason
string; the answer (click the value) lives in the table far below. `DEFAULT` is drawn in
error red, louder than `ASSUMED` amber; `DERIVED` reuses the DEFAULT class (`badge()`,
`:2132`) so a closure-forced wall and a house default look identical. The order of attention
should be ASSUMED > DERIVED > DEFAULT and the page inverts it.

**W4 — The transcriber's judgment calls never reach the sheet.** `takeoff.json` carries nine
`interpretations` for this case, including "if the NE heater runs 12" along the wall the
total is 17'5", not 17'8" — needs a tape or a client answer". `html_report` never references
`interpretations` (observed by grep, `:2392-2607`), so the single most useful sentence for
Gabe is only in the JSON. The three amber "INTERPRETATION — CONFIRM" cards are actually the
room's `notes` (pen transcriptions) mislabelled.

**W5 — Two things the sheet says are no longer true, and one thing it should say is missing
(audit D-4, D-6).** "Each of these builds with an ASSUMED note placed in the model" — false
since `NOTES_IN_MODEL = false` at 1.17.0. The door row shows width / at / height and no
hinge; an assumed hinge (`flag_inventory`) appears nowhere a reviewer looks (observed in
lane D's hinge-probe).

**W6 — The closure warning is inserted at the top of the room (`sec.insertBefore(cw,
sec.children[1])`, JS), above the photo — not where the edit was made.** Edit a wall in the
table and the warning appears two screens up.

**W7 — The phone does not work.** At a true 390-px viewport (`current-phone-390.png`,
observed via the iframe harness) the stated-values table, the OK cards and the interpretation
cards overflow past the right edge and their text is cut; the plan is a fixed 560-px SVG
that scrolls inside its box; the photo is 350 px wide with pen numbers ~3 px tall. Gabe
photographs plans on his phone; today the sheet is desktop-only.

**W8 — The 3D viewer costs 340 px per room and its labels collide** ("door heater 3'-0"
2'-9"" overprinted, observed). For a rectangle it tells Gabe nothing the plan does not; for
the UIC L-room with a bulkhead it does earn its place.

**W9 — STEP 1 and STEP 2 do not exist; only "STEP 3" is labelled.** The flow (check → answer
→ verdict → send) is implicit. The send-back text tells Gabe to "run
`python scripts/takeoff-check.py ... --apply-patch`" — that is the operator's job, not his,
and it is the one instruction on the page a draftsman cannot act on.

**W10 — As a document it does not look like something a shop would sign off.** No date, no
checker version, no transcriber; a file path where the job identity should be; 9.5-px
tracked uppercase labels everywhere; uniform cards with the same border for a passed check,
a pen note and the patch box, so nothing has rank.

Not wrong, and kept exactly: the patch contract, the `{v, src}` rule with `EDIT_SRC`, the
closure rule (warn, never rewrite the opposite wall), values editable on both plan and
list, the unit toggle re-rendering everything from `data-in`, the notes box on NEEDS
CHANGES, zoom, the floating edit popover, the theme handling, the `</`-escaped JSON blocks.

## 3. The proposed direction

One room is one **spread**: the evidence (photo and plan) is pinned on the left and stays
put, and a single **verification checklist** scrolls on the right — one row per value with a
tick, what the pen says, where it landed, and the editable number — with the never-measured
values collected in an amber "Answer these first" band inside the room that jumps to their
rows. Flags read in one hue at three weights (ASSUMED filled, DERIVED outlined, DEFAULT
dotted), measured values carry no badge at all, the closure warning appears under the walls
and paints the offending walls red on the plan, the checker's passes collapse to one green
block, the transcriber's judgment calls and the pen notes are shown as what they are, the
3D view opens on demand, the verdict sits at the foot of the room next to "what is still
unanswered", and the send-back box says in Gabe's terms what happens after he pastes. On a
phone the same page is one column with the photo pinned as a tap-to-enlarge strip and
nothing scrolling sideways.

## 5. What stays untouched and why

- **The patch contract** (`patch / job / review / edits[{room, field, old, new{v, src}}] /
  notes`), `EDIT_SRC = "stated corrected on the review sheet"`, `old` fixed at generation
  and matched at display precision — Benton's requirement 1 and 2 of 31 Aug; `--apply-patch`
  and the selftest depend on it. The mockup emits exactly this shape.
- **One value, every view** — `data-field` registry, edits repaint list and plan together.
- **Closure warns, never rewrites** — same `closureIssues` rule (N vs S, E vs W, parts sum);
  only where it is shown changes.
- **The unit toggle** re-rendering every number from inches; **zoom**; the **floating
  popover**; the **notes box** behaviour; the **lightbox**.
- **The grammar** — the sheet keeps embedding the dialog's `parseLen`/`arch` verbatim
  (`dialog_grammar_js`). The mockup pastes the same two functions and says so.
- **Theme handling** — bare `:root` light, guarded media query, `[data-theme]` blocks.
- **Declined on 31 Aug and still declined**: booth fit check, version diff, wall/scene
  picker. The **ASSUMED-list-as-field-checklist** was a printable list to take a tape to;
  the ticks in C8 are a desk aid that never leaves the page, which is why I think it is a
  different thing — but it is Q2 below, not a decision I made.

## 6. Build plan for `scripts/takeoff-check.py`, in slices

Each slice is one VERSION bump, verified with `--selftest`, `#autotest` in headless Chrome,
and a look at both harnesses in `.forge/scoper/takeoff-sheet/` (desktop and the 390-px
iframe). The mockup is the visual target; its CSS tokens and class names are meant to be
lifted.

1. **Data slice (small, unblocks the rest).** `load_photos`/`room_images`: match any
   filename in `sources` (C20). Stamp `generated`, `checker_version` into the lock (C1).
   Render `flag_inventory` (C17). Drop the in-model-note sentence (C16). Selftest cases:
   a `sources: ["plan.png"]` job embeds; a hinge-probe sheet contains "ASSUMED" beside the
   hinge. Files: `scripts/takeoff-check.py` (`load_photos`, `_img_of`, `check_file`,
   `html_report`, `selftest`).
2. **Skeleton slice.** New `CSS`; masthead, sticky strip, job line, room spread grid,
   sticky evidence column, verdict bar, send-back box (C1–C4, C14, C15, C18). Keep every
   existing `data-*` hook so `JS` works unchanged. Verify `#autotest` still passes and the
   390-px harness shows no horizontal overflow.
3. **Checklist slice.** Replace `room_ledger` + the stated-values table with one row
   generator (C8, C9), the "Answer these first" band (C7), checks block (C11), pen notes and
   interpretations (C12). Ticks: page-local state feeding the strip counts and the verdict
   line. Selftest: the patch emitted by `#autotest` still applies.
4. **Plan slice.** `_svg_room`: door `at` dimension, leaf + arc from `hinge`, north arrow,
   wall names, legend, closure walls in red (C6, C10); `JS`: closure box moves under the
   Walls group; row hover → plan highlight. Verify on `synthetic-jog` (L-room) and the UIC
   fixture (bulkhead, declared ccw) that labels do not collide.
5. **3D + phone slice.** Viewer behind a toggle, initialised on first open (C13); phone
   rules (C5, C19). Verify at 390 via the harness on the UIC three-room sheet.
6. **Docs slice** (outside `takeoff-check.py`): `SKILL.md` STOP command (C21),
   `reference/takeoff-format.md` "review sheet" section re-described, DEVLOG entry.

Acceptance for the whole: on `clients/uic-daley-library` and `eval/floorplans/blind-f-mech`
the sheet (a) embeds the photo for both `IMG_7594.jpeg` and `photo.png` names, (b) shows
every value exactly once in the checklist and once on the plan, (c) shows the hinge and the
interpretations, (d) emits a patch `--apply-patch` accepts, (e) has no horizontal page
scroll at 390 px, (f) `#autotest` passes, (g) contains no external resource.

## 7. Questions for Benton

- **Q1 — 3D view: collapsed by default (mockup), always open, or open only for non-rectangular
  rooms?** You picked the viewer on 31 Aug; I am proposing to hide it one click away because
  for a rectangle it repeats the plan and its labels collide. Recommend: collapsed.
- **Q2 — Ticks.** The per-row "checked against the photo" boxes are page-local in the mockup
  and never enter the patch. Should the tick count ride in the patch (e.g. `"checked":
  {"mech": 12}`) so the operator can see a room was approved with only 4 of 12 values
  looked at? That is a small contract change; the 31 Aug decline was about a field
  checklist, so I did not assume. Recommend: keep it page-local for now.
- **Q3 — Interpretations per room or per job?** They are one top-level list today. The
  mockup shows them inside the room because there is one room; on UIC (three rooms) they
  would sit under the masthead unless the format gains an optional per-room key.
  Recommend: render at job level now, add the per-room key later if it earns it.
- **Q4 — Photo embedding default.** With C20 the sheet embeds whatever `sources` names when
  `--embed-photos` is given. Do you want the skill to have the agent ask you per client, or
  a per-client `embed_photos: true` in takeoff.json so it is recorded once? Recommend: the
  key in the file, asked once.
- **Q5 — Transcriber name.** Adding `transcribed_by` to takeoff.json (agent / Gabe / Benton)
  so the masthead can say who read the plan. Yes / no.
