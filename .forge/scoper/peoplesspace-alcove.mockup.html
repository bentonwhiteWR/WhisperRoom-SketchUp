What the fragment does not tell us

  • What is east of the alcove line — the ramp toe and the open door both stand 1½–2½ ft out there in every option.
  • The raised floor: thickness, whether the booth (3,100 lb Enhanced) sits on it, and whether the 0'-0" datum is its top or the slab.
  • The pipes' actual plan position and diameter — drawn here as a 21" band off the concrete wall from a pixel read of the elevation.
  • The wall grille on the west wall: bottom height (~8'-7 ¾" by pixel read), projection, and whether it is supply or return — it sits over the roof unit.
  • Whether the glass wall carries a door at its east end (the photo suggests one) and where its swing goes — it matters for option 2.
  • Wall thicknesses (cosmetic here), the concrete wall's east return, and anything about power, the fire-suppression kit location, or how the roof unit exhausts.
