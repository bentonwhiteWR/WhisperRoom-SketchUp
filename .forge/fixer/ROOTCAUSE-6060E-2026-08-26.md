# MDL 6060 E inner shell — root cause and fix, 2026-08-26

**UNRUN IN SKETCHUP.** Nothing below has been built. It is a Python replay of the
Ruby placement maths against the real layout data, plus a `rbparse` syntax check.

## Root cause, one sentence

`rebalance_walls` re-walks a wall from each part's real width, and for an `ENH`
part — where `wall_slab` finds no panel — the only width it had was the whole
definition's **packaged bounding box**, which overran the 6060 E's E inner wall by
0.250 in, past the 0.15 closure tolerance, so that wall silently bailed out and kept
its stale slot polygons; `place()` then centred the 35.75-long `ENH 35.5VNT` on the
11.5 slot and put it at booth y **−7.875**, outside the booth.

## Reproduction (before any change)

`.forge/fixer/replay-rebalance.py` reimplements `rebalance_walls` and `place()`'s
along-wall arithmetic against `scripts/wr-booth-data.rb`. Under the CURRENT rule it
reproduces **all 16 of Benton's probed positions exactly** (probe converted with
booth x = X0+1.75, booth y = Y0−7.875):

| slot | harness, current rule | probe → booth |
|---|---:|---:|
| **E1i** `ENH 35.5VNT` | **−7.8750** | **−7.875** |
| E0i | 34.2500 | 34.250 |
| E-seal0i | 12.8750 | 12.875 |
| W1i | 4.2500 | 4.250 |
| W0i | 46.3750 | 46.375 |
| W-seal0i | 37.0000 | 37.000 |
| N0i / N1i / N-seal0i | 4.1250 / 46.2500 / 36.8750 | same |
| S0i / S1i / S-seal0i | 4.2500 / 46.2500 / 36.8750 | same |

The four corner seals are placed directly from the part's authored frame and were
already exact.

## The mechanism, wall by wall

`pw_of` returns, for a slab-less part, the bounding box if it differs from the slot
by more than `SLAB_NOISE` (1.0). The E/W swap in `ASSIGN` makes every inner E/W part
differ by ~24 in, so the box is always used. The boxes carry packaging:

| part | module | box | packaging |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `ENH 35.5VNT` | 35.5 | 35.750 | +0.250 |
| `ENH 35.5PanelSolid` | 35.5 | 35.625 | +0.125 |
| `ENH 11.5PanelSolid` | 11.5 | 11.500 | 0 |

- **E inner:** 4.25 + 35.750 + 6.5 + 11.5 = 58.000 against a wall ending at 57.75 →
  off +0.250 → **bails out**, polys stay stale → the floating panel.
- **W inner:** 4.25 + 35.625 + 6.5 + 11.5 = 57.875 → off +0.125 → squeaks under the
  tolerance and rebalances **1/8 long**. That is why W-seal0i landed at 37.0 instead
  of 36.875 (Defect 2) — the seal shift came out +24.125 instead of +24.000.
- **Both outer walls:** Standard parts have a findable slab, so the re-walk uses the
  exact panel widths 40 + 2 + 16 and closes to +0.0000. **The outer shell of this
  model is not affected** — checked, not assumed.

## The fix, and why it is here and not in the data

Two edits, both in `scripts/build-booth-components.rb`:

1. **New `self.iep_nominal_width(name)`** — the module width the part's name
   declares (`/ENH\s+([\d.]+)/`, the same regex `iep_room_proud` already uses).
   `rebalance_walls`'s `pw_of` now prefers it over `cls[:w]` when there is no slab.
   That is the width the layout polygons are cut to and the width the Standard slab
   measures on the same wall, so the inner shell now rebalances exactly as well as
   the outer one already did.
2. **New `IEP_VENT_YAW = 180.0`**, applied to inner vent panels about their slot
   polygon's centre, in the same style as `IEP_SEAL_YAW` / `IEP_DOOR_YAW`.

**Not the generator, not `ASSIGN`, not `wr-booth-data.rb`:**

- `wr-booth-data.rb` is header-marked GENERATED; a hand edit is overwritten by the
  next `gen-booth.py --all`.
- Baking the swap into `gen-booth.py` would move the E/W swap out of the one place
  documented to own it (the long comment above `ASSIGN`), and would rewrite four
  booths × two shells with only one of them probed.
- `ASSIGN` is Benton's recorded intent — the 40/35.5 part and the vent both at the
  door end — and the code's own design already says `rebalance_walls` is the
  mechanism that makes the geometry follow it. It worked on the outer shell. It
  failed on the inner one for exactly one reason, and that reason is now fixed.

## After the fix (same harness, `use_nominal`)

- 6060 E: E and W inner both close **+0.0000**. E1i `ENH 35.5VNT` → 4.1250..39.8750
  (inside the booth, its 1/8 packaging symmetric about the 4.25..39.75 slot);
  E0i → 46.2500..57.7500. W identical. **All four mid-wall seals land at 36.875**,
  matching N and S — the symmetry a square booth must have.
- 6084 E, 7272 E, 7296 E inner walls: were bailing or stretching, now close +0.0000.
- **MDL 4872 E: byte-identical before and after.** No wall reaches the changed line
  (`need` is false, because every ENH box is within `SLAB_NOISE` of its own slot).
  The 0.0001 regression is untouched.
- **Standard-only path: byte-identical.** Standard parts return early on their slab.

## Defects re-derived, not patched

- **Defect 2 (`W-seal0i` at 37.0)** — not an independent fault. It is the +0.125
  stretch above, and it goes to 36.875 with Defect 1 fixed. No number was patched.
- **Defect 4 (thin-box 11.5, "opposite sign N vs S")** — **not a defect.** Working it
  through with the probe's own RUN of 1.1563 (not the nominal 1.125):
  - S1i: `(pys.max + proud) − wy.max` → box max y = 4.25 + 0.0625 = 4.3125, so
    box min y = 4.3125 − 1.1563 = **3.1562** = the probe.
  - N1i: `pys.min − proud` → box min y = 57.75 − 0.0625 = **57.6875** = the probe.

  Both sit 1/16 **into the room**, the same sign, exactly as the rule says. The prior
  analysis used 1.125 for the box and got 1/32 on S. Nothing was changed here.
  What remains open is whether 1/16 is right for a 1.156-thick box in a 2.0 band —
  that is Defect 5's missing measurement, not a sign error.
- **Defect 5 (unmeasured room-proud on 11.5 and 35.5)** — untouched, as instructed.
  Both still fall through to `IEP_ROOM_PROUD_DEFAULT` and the build still warns.

## Residual risk

- **`IEP_VENT_YAW` also flips the 4872 E's `N0i`.** A 180 about the slot centre,
  followed by the room-proud re-seat, leaves the bounding box exactly where it was —
  which is why the 4872's 0.0001 probe agreement cannot see it, and equally why that
  agreement was never evidence the vent faced the right way. Only Benton's eye
  closes this.
- The harness models the along-wall axis. The across-wall re-seat and the z stack are
  unchanged code and were not replayed.
- `ENH 17.5PanelSolid` and `ENH 41.5Panel3236WDO` box widths in the harness are
  **assumed** at the panel family's +0.125. They only affect the 7272/7296 "before"
  columns, not the fix and not the 4872.
