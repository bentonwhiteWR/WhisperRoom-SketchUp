# MDL 6060 E — inner-shell placement probe, 2026-08-26

Benton built **MDL 6060 E, Shell = Inner (IEP) only** on plugin 1.6.20 and probed the
16 inner instances. Raw probe is pasted at the bottom. His verdict: *"Its quite broken.
Easy fixes, the vent walls need to rotate 180 degrees."*

## How to read the probe's columns (established, not assumed)

- `X0/Y0/Z0` are **relative to the selection's own low corner**, which the header puts at
  `1.7500, -7.8750, 0.7500` in the booth group's space. So
  **booth x = X0 + 1.75, booth y = Y0 − 7.875, booth z = Z0 + 0.75.**
  Verified: all four corner seals convert to exactly their layout polys.
- `RUN` is **extent along the walk axis (Y)**, not the part's width. For an N/S wall part
  that is its thickness. `RUN 2.375` on `ENH 35.5VNT` is its bbox thickness, not a defect —
  do not chase it.

## What is CORRECT — do not touch

Converted to booth coordinates and compared against the `'MDL 6060 E'` block in
`scripts/wr-booth-data.rb`, these land on their layout polys exactly:

| part | booth low corner | layout poly low corner |
|---|---|---|
| SW / SE / NW / NE corner seal i | (1.75,1.75) (54.875,1.75) (1.75,54.875) (54.875,54.875) | identical |
| N-seal0i | (36.875, 57.75) | identical |
| S-seal0i | (36.875, 1.75) | identical |
| E-seal0i | (57.75, 12.875) | identical |
| S0i door | x 4.25 exact; y 2.7253 | the accepted 4872 door figure (DEVLOG 2026-08-25) |

All four corners, three of the four mid-wall seals, and the door's along-wall position are
right. The corner transform, the 6.5 stem and the 0.75 lift all survive this booth.

## DEFECT 1 — E and W panels are in each other's slots (the root cause)

`ASSIGN['MDL 6060 E']` (`scripts/build-booth-components.rb:467`) says:

```
'E0i' => 'ENH 11.5PanelSolid',   'E1i' => 'ENH 35.5VNT',
'W0i' => 'ENH 11.5PanelSolid',   'W1i' => 'ENH 35.5PanelSolid'
```

But the layout polys say the opposite about which slot is LONG:

| slot | poly run | ASSIGN gives it |
|---|---:|---|
| E0i | y 22.25–57.75 = **35.5** | `ENH 11.5PanelSolid` |
| E1i | y 4.25–15.75 = **11.5** | `ENH 35.5VNT` |
| W0i | y 22.25–57.75 = **35.5** | `ENH 11.5PanelSolid` |
| W1i | y 4.25–15.75 = **11.5** | `ENH 35.5PanelSolid` |

**`place()` centres a component on its slot polygon's centre.** Proof: slot E1i's centre is
y (4.25+15.75)/2 = 10.0; a 35.75-long part centred there starts at 10.0 − 17.875 = **−7.875**,
and the probe reports E1i at booth y exactly **−7.875**. That single number confirms the
mechanism and is why the selection bbox is 68.125 long instead of ~53.5, and why a panel
floats outside the booth in the screenshot.

The same mismatch exists on the **outer** shell of this model (`'E0' => '16PanelSolid'` against
a 40-long E0 poly). The probe was inner-only so there is **no evidence either way** about how
the outer behaves — check it, do not assume it is fine and do not assume it is broken.

**The intent is recorded in the ASSIGN comment** (line ~406): *"E0 is the layout's vent slot, so
the vent moves with the 40 to the door end."* So the physical intent is the 40/35.5 part AND the
vent both at the door (south) end. The names in ASSIGN express that; the layout geometry was
never regenerated to match. **Fix the disagreement so the long part lands in a long slot** — the
slot geometry is what is stale, not Benton's intent. Flag which file you changed and why.

## DEFECT 2 — W-seal0i is on the wrong half of the W wall

Probe converts to booth y **37.0**; its layout poly is y **12.875–25.125**. The other three
mid-wall seals are exact. 37.0 is near the mirror of 12.875 about the booth's y centreline
(36.875) but off by 0.125. Likely the W seal follows the placed panel junction rather than its
own poly, so it may fall out once Defect 1 is fixed — **re-derive it, do not patch the number.**

## DEFECT 3 — the vents face the wrong way (Benton, by eye)

*"the vent walls need to rotate 180 degrees."* Probe yaws: `N0i ENH 35.5VNT` 180, and the
E-wall vent 90. This is Benton's direct instruction and is independent of Defect 1.
Whatever you change must keep the **4872 E** vents correct — that booth is closed and verified
to 0.0001.

## DEFECT 4 — the thin-box 11.5 is seated inconsistently N vs S

`ENH 11.5PanelSolid` is 1.125 thick where the family is 2.0625 — one of the four known
thin-box parts.

- `S1i` booth y 3.1562 in the 2.25–4.25 band → its room face sits ~1/32 past the band's room
  face (4.25).
- `N1i` booth y 57.6875 in the 57.75–59.75 band → **1/16 proud of the room face**, i.e. it
  sticks INTO the room, the opposite sign.

Same part, same nominal treatment, two different results. Suspect the thin-box across-wall
rule is applied in world sign rather than in the part's own frame.

## DEFECT 5 — unmeasured room-proud, expected and not yet fixable

`N0i` lands at booth (4.125, 57.625) against a poly corner of (4.25, 57.75) — 0.125 out on
both axes. `IEP_ROOM_PROUD` (line 164) has measured figures only for `17.5`, `41.5` and the
vent family; **11.5 and 35.5 fall through to `IEP_ROOM_PROUD_DEFAULT`**, exactly as the build
warning says. Do NOT invent numbers for them. Fix defects 1–4, and the residual on the next
probe is the measurement Benton has to give.

## Out of scope for this fix

- The IEP deck. `ENH 6060FL` / `ENH 6060CL` do not exist; the library ships 6042 + 6018
  SIDE L/R and the tiling rule is unanswered by Benton. The builder correctly refuses by name.
- Any change to the 4872 E result, or to the Standard-only path.
