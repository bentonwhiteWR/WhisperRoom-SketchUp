## The room — all six walls as measured

**Every dimension below is confirmed by Benton.** The room is **not square**.

| Wall | Inches | Feet |

| Short side (far end) | 336½ | 28'-0½" |
| Door side | 195¼ | 16'-3¼" |
| Outside the corner room | 563¼ | 46'-11¼" |

Open floor **1,432 sq ft**, corner room a further 77 sq ft. Ceiling **~27 ft** to an
open painted deck.

### The room is 1° out of square

Across the corner-room end: **195¼ + 151¼ = 346½**.
At the far end: **336½**.

Ten inches. That is the room, not a bad measurement — the long walls converge by
**1.0° over 47 ft**.

Drawn with all six figures as measured, **one residual** remains: the wall outside the
corner room computes to 561¾ against the 563¼ written, **1½ in over 47 ft**. Ordinary
tape error at that distance. Every other wall closes exactly.

**Booth consequence:** a booth is square and the room is not. Along the long wall the
gap behind it tapers by up to 10 in end to end. Easy to live with, but plan it rather
than discover it, and it decides which end you set out from.
